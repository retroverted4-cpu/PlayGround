How to pop balloons

1

Pick your monkey

Choose a tower from the armory. Each one has a unique way of turning balloons into confetti.

2

Place on the grass

Click or tap open grass beside the path. The green ring shows range; red means it is too close to the track or an obstacle.

3

Send the round

Tap Start Round when you are ready. Survive all 30 to save the island. Leaked balloons cost lives.

4

Upgrade & adapt

Select any monkey to buy upgrades, change its targeting, or sell it back. Layered balloons release their contents when popped.
